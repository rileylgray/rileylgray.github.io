What's inside

Build the maze

There are no fixed roads: the ants walk wherever you let them. Every tower is also a wall, so the trail twists around your defenses — build it long and every tower gets more shots.

Drag, drop, detour

Drag a tower onto the grass and see the ants' new trail before you let go. Paint whole walls of pebbles with one swipe, and sell towers back in full until the next wave starts.

10 kinds of ants

Speedy sugar ants, armored soldiers, swarming army ants, flying ants that skip your maze, heatproof fire ants, nurses that heal the others and more — each one needs a plan.

10 picnic-gear towers

Straw shooters, syrup squirters, pepper grinders, fly swatters, bug zappers, magnifying glasses, soda cannons and cookie jars that bake bonus crumbs. Upgrade each up to four levels.

Beat the Ant Queen

Boss waves send the enormous Ant Queen marching on your picnic. Stomp and Freeze powers help out when things get close to the blanket.

Hard & Endless

Beat the Backyard to unlock Hard mode, with more and tougher ants and its own stars. Clear a world's six levels and its Endless mode opens — survive as long as you can.

The Pantry

Spend honey on permanent perks like sharper aim, longer reach and cheaper towers. Master your favorite towers for a fourth level, collect six picnic blankets and claim daily gifts.

Play offline

1x, 2x and 3x speed, pause and keep building any time, and the game saves at every wave. Plays fully offline on phones and tablets. Free, with optional ads.

Five picnic spots

Backyard

Where it all begins. Keep the ants off the blanket.

City Park

Ponds, trees and flying ants.

Beach

Tide pools, sandcastles and carpenter ants.

Campsite

Logs, creeks and the Ant Queen herself.

Mountain Meadow

The last picnic. Everything they have.

Each spot has six levels with up to three stars on every one — 30 levels in all, plus an Endless mode for each world. Grab a pebble. The picnic isn't going to protect itself!
