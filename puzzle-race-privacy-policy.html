Privacy Policy for Puzzle Race

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Puzzle Race ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") is a multiplayer puzzle game for Android and iOS in which players race each other to solve the same puzzle. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

Puzzle Race is operated by Riley Gray (Real Games Real Fun). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at:

Email: realgamesrealfun@outlook.com

2. Information We Collect

Account and profile

  • Player ID. When you first open the app we create an anonymous account with a random identifier. You never have to give us your real name.
  • Racer name and avatar. The display name, avatar and cosmetics you choose. These are shown to other players in races, private rooms and leaderboards.
  • Email address (optional). Only if you choose to link an email in Settings → Account, so you can restore your progress on another device or reset your password.

Game data

  • Level, XP, coins, in-game items you unlock with coins, ratings, statistics, match history, daily challenge completion, and the moves you make in online races (used to verify results and prevent cheating).
  • Solo practice progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Device, diagnostics and usage

  • Device and app information (such as model, operating system version and app version), crash reports and performance diagnostics.
  • Gameplay events such as "match started" or "puzzle type played", used to understand how the game is played.
  • App integrity signals used to confirm requests come from the genuine app.

Advertising

  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. The app does not sell any real-money products; there are no in-app purchases.

3. How We Use Information

  • To run the game: matchmaking, live races, private rooms, rewards, rankings and leaderboards.
  • To save your progress and let you restore it on another device if you link an email.
  • To verify race results and detect cheating, fraud and abuse.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability and gameplay.
  • To show ads, including personalised ads only where you have given consent where it is required.

For users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, our legal bases are performance of our contract with you (providing the game), our legitimate interests (security, anti-cheat, crash analysis and product improvement), and your consent (personalised advertising and, on iOS, tracking).

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following services provided by Google, which process data on our behalf and under their own terms:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check and Remote Config) — Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (consent management) — How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Information Shared With Other Players

Your racer name, avatar, cosmetics, level, rating and leaderboard positions are visible to other players. Your email address and player account identifiers are never shown to other players. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising Choices

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states) we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS, we ask your permission through App Tracking Transparency before accessing the advertising identifier. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. Rewarded ads are always optional.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account and game data are kept for as long as your account exists. Live race records are removed from our servers within 6 hours of the race ending (your personal match history stays in your account), daily challenge data after 7 days, and weekly leaderboards after 3 weeks.

You can permanently delete your account and all associated server data at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete my account, or by emailing realgamesrealfun@outlook.com with your Player ID (shown in Settings → Account). We process email requests within 30 days. Crash reports and analytics data are kept by Google for the retention periods set in their services (up to 14 months for analytics).

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to our servers is protected by authentication, app integrity checks and strict database security rules, and game-critical data such as results, coins and rankings is written only by our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Children's Privacy

Puzzle Race is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, or to withdraw consent. You can change your name and avatar in the app at any time. To exercise any other right, contact us at realgamesrealfun@outlook.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, by a notice in the app. Your continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
