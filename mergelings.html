Mergelings

A cosy merge game about growing a meadow full of little creatures. Drag two matching friends together and they merge into something you have never seen before. Drag, merge, discover, repeat.

Sneak Peek

What's inside

Drag to merge

A basket at the edge of your meadow fills all by itself and drops a new friend. Drag two matching friends together to merge them into the next species — or tap the basket to hurry it along.

150 creatures

Every one of them is its own creature — no two silhouettes are alike, and they stay crisp on any screen. Watch your collection fill in as you discover them.

Hearts every second

Every friend standing in your meadow earns hearts around the clock, and they keep adding up for four hours while the app is closed. Come back to what your meadow made.

Mystery baskets

Open a mystery basket for a friend far above your usual luck. Expand your meadow with extra rows, or sell friends you no longer need and put the hearts toward something better.

Dress your favourites

Flower crowns, bow ties, party hats, cosy scarves, top hats, sunglasses, hero capes, headphones and golden crowns. An accessory is yours forever and stays with the species that wears it.

Made to be restful

No way to lose, no lives to run out of, and no timer between you and the next merge. A soft pastoral loop plays for hours without seaming, and sound, music and vibration each have their own switch.

Play anywhere

Your meadow lives on your device, so it works with no connection at all. Portrait only, one-handed, and quick to pick up — in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German, with all 150 names translated.

Fair about free

Free and supported by advertising. Rewarded videos are always optional and always offered up front — you never have to watch one to keep playing, and you will never hit a wall that only a video gets you past.

Five meadows to fill

Day Meadow

Sun-warmed grass and woodland friends.

Night Meadow

Moonlit critters and starry wanderers.

Sea Meadow

Reef shallows down to the deep blue.

Myth Meadow

Folklore, legend and elder dragons.

Dino Meadow

Fossil giants from a lost world.

Each meadow is its own merge chain of 30 creatures, with its own colours, its own sky and its own music-box calm. Reach far enough into one and the next one opens.
