Couple Life

One shared space for you and your partner — groceries, chores, notes, reminders, a calendar and a simple budget, plus a few features to keep things fun. Works solo too; your partner can join any time with a 6-character invite code.

Get it on Google Play

Coming soon to the App Store.

What's inside

Shared space, instantly

Create a space, share a 6-character invite code like K7PW3M, and everything syncs in real time. Fully usable solo until a partner joins.

Groceries & chores

Quick-add grocery lists you tick off together, plus assignable chores with due dates and repeats — spin the wheel to fairly pick who's up.

Notes & reminders

Pinboard-style shared notes with colors and pinning, plus a shared reminder list with dates, times and overdue highlighting.

Shared calendar

A shared month calendar for all-day or timed events, with events and reminders together in one tab.

Budget & settle-up

Log shared expenses with categories and who paid, then see this month's total and a settle-up balance based on your configurable split.

Little bits of fun

Send emoji nudges to your partner, spin the wheel of chores, and track your days together with an anniversary countdown.

Built with Flutter and Firebase for real-time sync, localized into 6 languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese). Available now on Google Play, with an App Store release on the way.
