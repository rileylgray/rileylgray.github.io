Sneak Peek

What's inside

Grab, fling & rip

Drag any body part and hurl the Dummy across the room. Grab with two fingers and pull, and arms, legs and even the head come right off.

46 crazy weapons

Bats, katanas and chainsaws, a minigun and a nail gun, dynamite and a nuke, falling pianos, a black hole, a charging bull and a shark — each one drops into the room to pick up.

A dummy with feelings

It blinks, flinches, sees stars, sweats when you pick something scary and laughs when you leave it alone. Set it on fire and it runs round the room screaming.

Two's company

Unlock a second Dummy and they bump, shove and laugh at each other, throw loose limbs back and forth and poke a fallen friend back to its feet.

Dress it up

Crash Dummy, Human, Zombie, Robot or Alien, with hair, glasses, suits, hoodies and clown shoes in any colour. Dress it as your boss in one tap.

Four rooms

Wreck the Garage, the Office, the Backyard or the Moon Lab, where low gravity sends everything floating.

Earn as you smash

Every hit earns coins for new weapons, outfits and rooms. Chain combos, fill the stress meter for a payout and watch a slow-mo replay of every knockout.

Play offline

No account needed and it plays fully offline on phones and tablets. Vibration, screen shake and damage numbers can all be switched off. Free, with optional ads.

Pick your weapon

Hands

Boxing glove, bat, sledgehammer, katana, chainsaw, frying pan, a big wet fish, golf club, axe and mace.

Guns

Pistol, shotgun, minigun, laser, nail gun, crossbow, a bow you draw with two fingers, paintball, sniper and grenade launcher.

Explosives

Grenades, dynamite, a rocket launcher, sticky C4, fireworks, a cluster bomb and the mighty NUKE.

Drops

Bowling balls, anvils from the sky, throwing knives, a runaway saw blade and a piano.

Powers

Lightning, a flamethrower, a freeze ray, a black hole, a meteor and a tornado that chases it round the room.

Animals

A swarm of bees, a school of piranhas, a charging bull and a shark that bursts up out of the floor.

Traps

Crank them by hand: a stretcher, a vise that squashes it flat and an electric chair.

Cartoon violence, made for teens and adults. The Dummy spills fluffy stuffing to start with — switch blood on in Settings if you want the splatter.
