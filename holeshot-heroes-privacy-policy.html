Privacy Policy for Holeshot Heroes: Mini Golf

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Holeshot Heroes: Mini Golf ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") is a physics mini golf game for Android and iOS that you can play on your own, against computer players, or online with friends in private rooms. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and with whom it is shared.

The app is free and supported by ads. It has no in-app purchases. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

Holeshot Heroes is operated by Riley Gray.

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at:

Email: realgamesrealfun@outlook.com

2. Accounts

You do not need to create an account. You never have to enter an email address, password or phone number to play.

When your device is online, the app signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier (an "anonymous user ID") so your progress can be saved and restored securely. It is not linked to your real-world identity.

You can choose to link a Google or Apple account so you can keep your progress across devices. If you do, we receive the basic account information that sign-in provider shares (an account identifier and, depending on the provider and your choices, your name and email address). We use it only to sign you in and restore your progress.

3. Data We Collect and Why

3.1 Game progress

What: your display name and avatar, level and experience points, Golf Coins, stars and best scores per course, unlocked power-ups and cosmetics, your equipped items, and game statistics (for example matches played and holes-in-one).

Why: to save your progress, give out rewards, and restore everything if you reinstall the app or switch devices. Your progress is kept on your device and, when you are online, synchronised with our backend.

3.2 Online rooms

When you create or join a private room with a room code, the other players in that room see your display name, avatar, equipped cosmetics and your in-match results. To run the match, the app sends your ball's shots and positions, power-up activations and scores to the other players in the room through our online service. Room data is used only to run the match.

There is no text or voice chat. Players can only send preset emotes. There is no public matchmaking: you only play with people who have your room code.

3.3 Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example which courses are completed, which power-ups are used, and how long matches last) so we can improve difficulty and balance. It uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information (device model, operating system version, app version, and the state of the app when it crashed) so we can fix bugs. These services may collect an app instance ID and your approximate location derived from your IP address.

3.4 Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: banners in menus, occasional full-screen ads between matches (never during play), and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a reward. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this information to show personalized ads and to prevent fraud.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads are requested. In some US states, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the app's settings.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission to track before requesting personalized ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

For information on how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3.5 Data stored on your device

Your settings (such as sound, music and vibration) and a working copy of your progress are stored on your device. You can play offline; your progress is synchronised the next time you are online.

3.6 What we do not collect

  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not take payments: there is nothing to buy with real money.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

  • Progress and online room data is stored using Google Firebase, which acts as our service provider.
  • All data sent by the app is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Security rules make sure only you (your user ID) can change your own profile. Rewards and balances are checked on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your synchronised progress is kept for as long as your account exists so you can restore it. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's settings ("Delete my data"), or by emailing realgamesrealfun@outlook.com with your player ID (shown in the app's settings). We delete it within 30 days. Crash reports and analytics data are kept by Google for the retention periods set in their services (up to 14 months for analytics).

7. Children's Privacy

Holeshot Heroes is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, or to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at realgamesrealfun@outlook.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
