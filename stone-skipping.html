What's inside

Tap for Perfect

Swipe up to throw, then tap as the stone meets its shadow. Land a Perfect to keep your speed, and chain them together for bigger and bigger combos.

Flips & tricks

Flip and twist the stone mid-air for bonus coins. Land the trick for a speed boost — land in the middle of one and you wipe out.

Steer the throw

Swipe left or right to steer around rocks and logs, and past lily pads and ducks, as the stone races down the lake.

14 stones

From a plain river pebble to sea glass, jade, moonstone and gold — plus a few stones that aren't really stones at all.

5 upgrades

Spend coins on Throwing Arm, Spin, Lift, Focus and Coin Bonus to throw further, keep more speed on every skip and land more Perfects.

Leaderboards

Every lake has boards for most skips, longest distance and most tricks in one throw. Names are generated for you, so there's nothing to type.

Play offline

The whole game plays with no connection; new bests upload when you're back online. In English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Japanese.

Daily gift

Free and supported by advertising. Come back each day for a gift, and double it with an optional video if you like — rewarded videos are never required.
