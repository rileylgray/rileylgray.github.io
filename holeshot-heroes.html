Sneak Peek

Two ways to play

Race Mode

2 to 4 players shoot at the same time. No waiting for turns: the first ball in the cup wins the hole.

Fast & chaotic

Classic Mode

Take turns against the clock and line up the perfect shot. Lowest total strokes wins.

Tactical

What's inside

Slingshot shots

Drag back from the ball, watch the power ring fill and the flight path appear, then let it fly. Easy to learn, hard to master.

Real physics

Every bounce, roll and splash is simulated. Slopes speed you up, sand grabs the ball and water costs a stroke.

Private rooms

Create a room and share a six-character code. Only friends with the code can join — no strangers.

Offline bots

No internet? Play Easy, Normal or Hard bots that follow the same physics and rules as you.

Power-ups

Sticky Ball, Super Bounce, Gravity Flip, Shield and more. Equip three and outsmart your rivals.

Fair by design

No real-money purchases. Power-ups unlock by playing, and cosmetics never affect your game.
