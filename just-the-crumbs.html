What's inside

Your recipe book

Create, edit and delete recipes with ingredients and step-by-step instructions. Search by title, drag to reorder, and tag with colored categories.

Import from a URL

Paste a recipe link — or a TikTok — and the app parses the ingredients and steps for you, then opens a pre-filled form to review and save.

Guest or Google

Cook right away as a guest, no account needed. Sign in with Google when you're ready and your guest recipes carry over automatically.

Discover

Browse a public feed of others' recipes, search, hit “surprise me” for a random pick, and filter by content language.

Share by code

Share any recipe with a short code and a tap-to-open link — works for private recipes too. Copy any shared recipe into your own collection.

Six languages

Fully localized in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian, with per-recipe language tags on the Discover feed.

Built with Flutter and backed entirely by Firebase (Auth + Cloud Firestore) — a serverless rewrite of the original web app. Available now on Google Play, with the App Store coming soon.
