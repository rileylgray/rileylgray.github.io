Sneak Peek

What's inside

Grab & fling

Touch any zombie to grab it with a real physics joint, then release to send it flying. Every throw feels satisfyingly squishy.

Six zombie types

Face normal, fast, tank, exploder, jumper, and spitter zombies — each with their own nasty tricks.

Powerups & upgrades

Unleash Lightning, Meteor, Bomb, and Piano powerups, then spend coins in the shop to upgrade your cabin and combos.

50 nights + Endless

Survive a 50-night campaign that gets harder every night, then take on Endless mode unlocked after Night 10.

Combo scoring

Chain kills to stack a multiplier and trigger DOUBLE, TRIPLE, and RAMPAGE call-outs for massive scores.

iOS & Android

Built with Flutter and Flame physics. Available now on the App Store, with Google Play on the way.
