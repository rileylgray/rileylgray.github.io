Delete your Puzzle Race account

Puzzle Race (by Real Games Real Fun) lets you permanently delete your account and the data linked to it at any time. Here's how.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open Puzzle Race.
  2. Tap the Settings button on the home screen.
  3. Open Account & sign-in.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you can't open the app, email realgamesrealfun@outlook.com with the subject "Puzzle Race account deletion". Include your Player ID (shown in Settings → Account & sign-in) or the email address you linked to your account. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details, including any linked email address
  • Your racer name, avatar, cosmetics and friend code
  • Your level, XP, coins, unlocked items, rating and statistics
  • Your match history and daily challenge progress
  • Your friends list and your entries on the global, this week's and per-puzzle leaderboards

What is kept, and for how long

  • Records of races still in progress or just finished are removed automatically within 6 hours.
  • Entries on previous weeks' leaderboards are removed automatically within 3 weeks.
  • Crash reports and analytics data are kept by Google Firebase for its standard retention periods (up to 14 months) and are not linked to your name.
  • Solo practice progress and settings stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.

See the Puzzle Race Privacy Policy for more details.
