Privacy Policy for Hubbub

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Hubbub: Online Party Games ("Hubbub", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") lets groups of 2 to 12 people play party games together, each on their own phone. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

By using Hubbub, you agree to the practices described in this policy and to our Terms of Use.

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

Hubbub is made and operated by Riley Gray (Real Games Real Fun).

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at:

Email: realgamesrealfun@outlook.com

2. No Accounts

Hubbub does not ask you to create an account, and it never asks for your email address, phone number, password, real name, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

So that rooms work securely, the app signs your device in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier (an "anonymous user ID") for your copy of the app. It is not linked to your real-world identity, and it lets the app know which player in a room is you.

3. Data We Collect and Why

3.1 Your nickname and avatar

What: The nickname you choose (up to 20 characters) and the avatar you build (an animal, a color and a hat).

Why: So the other players in your room can see who is who. Your nickname and avatar are saved on your device and sent to a room when you create or join it.

Please don't use your full name or any other personal information as your nickname.

3.2 What you send while playing in an online room

When you play in an online room, the app sends the following to the room:

  • your anonymous user ID, nickname and avatar,
  • whether you are ready and connected, and your score,
  • your game choices and votes,
  • what you create in the games: drawings, guesses, written answers, clues, bids, card plays and "likes",
  • chat messages you send in the room (up to 200 characters each; a room keeps its last 60 messages), and
  • reports you file and players the host has removed (so they cannot rejoin that room).

Why: To run the game. Everything you send to a room can be seen by the other people in that room, so only send what you are happy for them to see. Nicknames, guesses, answers and chat pass through a word filter, but no filter is perfect.

Retention: Rooms are temporary. Every room, and everything in it (including drawings, answers and chat), is automatically deleted from our servers within two days of being created.

3.3 Reports

If you report a player, the app saves a report for us to review. A report contains the room code, your anonymous user ID, the reported player's anonymous user ID and nickname, the reason you chose, up to the last five chat messages the reported player sent in the room, and the time.

Why: To moderate the app, act on abuse, and keep players safe. The host of the room is told that someone was reported, but not who reported them. Reports are not deleted with the room; we keep them for as long as we need them for moderation and safety.

3.4 Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how Hubbub is used, for example which screens are opened, how players get into rooms, which games are played, and whether invites are shared. Google Analytics also collects standard information such as an app instance ID, device model, operating system and app version, approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address), and session information.

Why: To find out what is working, fix problems, and decide what to improve. We do not use analytics to identify you personally.

3.5 Advertising

Hubbub is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the menus and, now and then, a full-screen ad between games. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID (the IDFA on iOS, if you allow it, or the Android advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location, device information, and how you interact with ads.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy choices" in the app's settings.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission to track before any ads are requested. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

For information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3.6 Data stored only on your device

Your nickname, your avatar, and the fact that you have accepted the house rules are stored on your device using standard on-device storage. Muting a player only hides their chat on your phone, for as long as you are in that room; they are not told, and nothing is sent to our servers.

Practice with Bots runs entirely on your phone. Nothing you draw or type in a practice game is sent to our servers.

4. How We Share Data

  • With the other players in your room, as described in Section 3.2.
  • With our service providers, who process data for us: Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions and Analytics).
  • With advertising partners: Google AdMob, and the ad partners Google works with, as described in Section 3.5.
  • When required by law, or when needed to protect the safety of our players or others.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Data

  • Room data and reports are stored in the Google Firebase Realtime Database, on Google's servers in the United States.
  • All data sent by the app is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS.
  • Database security rules let only signed-in copies of the app reach a room, and limit what each player can change.

If you use Hubbub from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

6. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Rooms (nicknames, avatars, drawings, answers, votes, scores and chat) are deleted automatically within two days.
  • Reports are kept for as long as we need them for moderation and safety.
  • On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Analytics and advertising data are kept by Google under its own retention policies.

Because Hubbub has no accounts, we cannot look up your data by your name or email. To ask us to delete data, email realgamesrealfun@outlook.com with as much detail as you can (for example the room code, your nickname and roughly when you played), and we will delete what we can find.

8. Children's Privacy

Hubbub is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time (for example from "Ad privacy choices" in the app). You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we use your data to provide the games you ask to play (to perform our agreement with you), to keep the app safe and improve it (our legitimate interests), and to show personalized ads (with your consent).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at realgamesrealfun@outlook.com.
